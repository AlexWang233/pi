Take a look at Pi Durable. It's a solid piece of work, but it's heavily framework-dependent and doesn't support multi-user interaction well; that is, multiple people talking with multiple agents simultaneously. Please propose a system design and implement a version of Pi Durable that supports multi-agent, interactive use of coding agents. To keep the scope manageable, you can assume that multiple people are working on the project simultaneously, and that everyone shares the same filesystem as long as they're in the same conversation thread.

You may find parts of T3 Code useful here, feel free to reuse its implementations of multiple agentic harnesses and providers directly. Once you have a working system, please stress-test it and analyze it for single points of failure, hotspots, and similar issues. Please design and deploy the system on Google Cloud Platform. GCP offers a $300 credit on sign-up, which should be more than enough for a proof of concept.
